# Customer Financial Distress Risk Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview
This dashboard analyzes customer financial and behavioral interaction data to surface early warning signals of potential financial distress. The goal is to support proactive risk identification and improve customer segmentation beyond traditional credit metrics.

The dashboard was built using Power BI and is based on a structured analytical dataset developed as part of an academic data analytics project.

---

## Business Question
How can customer interaction data be used to identify early indicators of financial distress and segment customers by risk level?

---

## Data Summary
- **Data type:** Transactional and behavioral interaction records
- **Entities:** Individual customers (masked identifiers)
- **Key variables:**
  - Financial stress indicators (e.g., liquidity risk, overdrafts, payment failures)
  - Behavioral stress indicators (e.g., repeated inquiries, usage changes, document requests)
  - Event timestamps for trend analysis

All data used is sample or academic data and does not contain sensitive or personally identifiable information.

---

## Dashboard Components
- **Key Metrics**
  - Total number of customers
  - Average stress events per customer
  - Overall stress score

- **Stress Frequency Analysis**
  - Distribution of financial stress categories
  - Distribution of behavioral stress indicators

- **Customer Risk Segmentation**
  - Classification of customers into low, medium, and high stress tiers

- **Time-Based Trends**
  - Visualization of stress events over time to identify escalation patterns

---

## Key Insights
- Liquidity-related events are the most frequent financial stress signals, indicating short-term cash pressure.
- Behavioral indicators such as repeated inquiries and usage changes often appear before severe financial stress events.
- A small subset of customers exhibits disproportionately high stress activity, suggesting elevated risk.
- Combining behavioral and financial indicators improves early detection compared to financial data alone.

---

## Business Value
This dashboard demonstrates how interaction data can be used to:
- Identify at-risk customers earlier
- Enable proactive outreach and intervention strategies
- Improve customer risk segmentation and monitoring
- Support data-informed decision-making for financial services teams
